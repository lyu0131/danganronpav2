Character Concept

Hifumi Yamada's design emphasizes his otaku identity and self-indulgent charm. The oversized cardigan and stretched dress shirt highlight his rounded silhouette, while the long tie with a bold arrow serves as a playful focal point. Twin backpacks suggest he is always carrying gadgets, snacks, and fan goods. Round glasses and curled forelock give him a distinctive profile and gentle comedic warmth. The warm browns, orange, and muted blues keep him approachable and grounded. Chunky sneakers and tapered pants add a school vibe and easy movement.

Every element of Hifumi's appearance communicates his complete immersion in otaku culture and creative passion. His layered, comfortable wardrobe reflects his belief that true worth comes from creative output, not appearance. The playful design details and accessible aesthetic make him instantly recognizable as a character uncompromising in his dedication to art and storytelling over superficial judgment.
