Character Concept

Monokuma is based on the idea of guilty (黒, literally "black") and innocent (白, literally "white"). His first beta design showcased him resembling an anatomy model commonly used in science courses, with his "white" and "black" sides originally representing a fully skinned side and one that exposes muscle and organs. As the style of the game changed, the character was made cuter and he became a teddy bear instead.

Another beta showcased Monokuma similarly to how he is now, with the exception of his bellybutton, the addition of paws with very notable claws, and a slimmer body. His current design doesn't show his claws. On early design sketches featuring both his slimmer and final self, Monokuma was represented driving a car, carrying knives, or using them to chop, showcasing a darker original vision that contrasts with his current cute appearance.
