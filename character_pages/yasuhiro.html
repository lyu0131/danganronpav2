Character Concept

Yasuhiro Hagakure's design emphasizes his laid back, offbeat personality and fortune teller image. His huge, unruly hair instantly makes him stand out and hints at someone scatterbrained and unpredictable. The loose layers of his rolled up shirt, open jacket, and tied pants give him a relaxed, wanderer-like silhouette, while the sandal-style footwear and beaded bracelet reinforce a spiritual, bohemian feel.

The muted earth tones keep him grounded and approachable, matching his role as a somewhat unreliable but oddly charming presence in the cast. Every element of Yasuhiro's appearance speaks to his free-spirited nature and connection to spirituality, creating a character who stands apart from his peers through both visual design and behavioral quirks.
