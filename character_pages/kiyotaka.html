Character Concept

Kiyotaka Ishimaru's design emphasizes discipline and unwavering order. The crisp white gakuran with brass buttons and bold red armband signal authority and rules, while the high collar and sharply pressed trousers form a rigid silhouette. Tall lace-up boots, a medal at the chest, and precise insignia reinforce a strict, institutional presence. Spiked hair and stern brows project intensity and conviction. The restrained white, black, and red palette keeps him clean and authoritative, presenting Taka as principled, uncompromising, and visually distinct from the looser styles of his peers.

Every element of his appearance communicates his foundational belief that structure and morality are inseparable. The institutional aesthetic of his uniform transforms him into a symbol of order itself. His meticulously maintained appearance reflects his conviction that discipline is a moral imperative. Kiyotaka's design represents the embodiment of a leader whose strength derives from unwavering principles and righteous commitment.
