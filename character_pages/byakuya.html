Character Concept

Byakuya Togami's design reflects his elite status and proud demeanor through refined details and a sophisticated color palette. His long coat and tailored attire convey authority and wealth, while his distinctive pale blonde hair and sharp facial features emphasize his cold, calculating nature. The dark navy and white color scheme gives him an aristocratic appearance that sets him apart from the other students. Every element of his design—from the intricate patterns on his coat to his composed posture—serves to reinforce his position as someone who considers himself above his peers, yet his youth betrays the internal struggles beneath his polished exterior.
