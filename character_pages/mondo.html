Character Concept

Mondo Owada's design centers on visual intensity, communicating his role as a fierce and intimidating delinquent leader. His long coat is heavily decorated with ornate patterns inspired by traditional gang and biker aesthetics, giving him a commanding silhouette and reinforcing his status. The bold dragon motifs and exaggerated belt buckle help emphasize his pride and explosive personality. His distinctive pompadour hairstyle is a major focal point, instantly conveying his aggressive attitude and old-school biker influence.

The deep color palette paired with gold detailing creates a striking contrast that fits his dramatic presence. Loose pants and visible layering give his outfit weight and movement, matching his physicality and imposing demeanor. While his design appears tough and overwhelming, smaller elements like the subtle purple lining hint at complexity beneath the surface—a duality that perfectly captures Mondo's true nature as a man bound by unwavering loyalty and honor despite his fearsome exterior.
